# KayaJob for Android

**Kaya mo 'yan.** The free all-in-one app for Filipino job seekers: track applications, prepare HR requirements, practice interviews with AI, know your real take-home pay, and get home safe with KayaNav.

## What's inside

- **Job tracker** with Overview, Job post and Details tabs, plus interview and follow-up reminders
- **Requirements vault** for SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, TIN, NBI and more
- **AI interview coach** with scores, tips and a stronger answer
- **Salary calculator** with SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG and tax, in pesos or 20+ currencies (monthly, daily or hourly), with the date its rates were last updated and an option to deduct them from foreign pay too
- **Compare offers** side by side: take-home after fares and rent, 13th month, bonuses, leaves, benefits and your asking salary
- **KayaNav** to plan the trip home with the last-trip warning, from any of your saved homes (owned or rented)
- **Scam checker** with job link and company checks, and a **resume builder** with PDF export

## Download

Get the latest APK from **[Releases](https://github.com/jedgarf/KayaJob-Releases/releases/latest)**.

**Requirements:** Android 7.0 or newer.

## How to install

1. Download `KayaJob-vX.Y.Z.apk` from the latest release on your phone.
2. Open it. If Android asks, allow installing apps from your browser or file manager.
3. If Play Protect warns you, tap **More details** → **Install anyway**. It shows this for apps installed outside the Play Store.
4. Tap **Install**, then open KayaJob and create your free account. You need internet to sign in and sync your data.

**Updating?** Install the new APK over the old one. Your data is saved to your account, so nothing is lost.

## Delete your account

Open **Settings → Delete account** in the app or on the web. Everything in your account is deleted right away. More details: [kayajob.vercel.app/delete-account](https://kayajob.vercel.app/delete-account).

## Verify your download (optional)

Each release lists the SHA-256 of its APK. To check it on a computer:

```bash
shasum -a 256 KayaJob-v1.0.0.apk
```
